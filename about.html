About Brick Breaker

Brick Breaker is a free, browser-based arcade game inspired by the classic Breakout (1976). Our goal is to deliver a polished, satisfying brick-breaking experience that loads instantly and plays smoothly on any device — no downloads, no sign-ups, no waiting.

What makes it special

  • 5 hand-crafted levels with distinct layouts: rows, pyramids, checkerboards, fortresses, and diamonds.
  • 5 power-ups that drop from bricks — wide paddle, multi-ball, slow motion, extra life, and laser.
  • Combo system rewarding skilled play with multiplied scores.
  • Particle effects & sound — every brick break feels satisfying with colorful debris and retro arcade beeps.
  • Local high score saved in your browser — no account needed.
  • Fully responsive — mouse, keyboard, and touch controls all supported.

How it's built

Brick Breaker is built with vanilla HTML5 Canvas and JavaScript — no game engine, no frameworks, no external dependencies. The entire game is under 15KB of code, meaning it loads almost instantly even on slow connections. Sound effects are generated in real-time using the Web Audio API, so there are zero audio files to download.
