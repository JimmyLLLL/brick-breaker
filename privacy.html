Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 23, 2026

This privacy policy explains how Brick Breaker ("we", "this site") handles visitor information. By using this site, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

Brick Breaker is a static browser game. We do not require registration and do not collect personal information such as your name, email, or phone number. The game stores your high score in your browser's local storage — this data never leaves your device.

2. Local Storage

The game uses your browser's local storage to save your high score between sessions. This data is stored only on your device and is not transmitted to any server. You can clear this data at any time through your browser settings.

3. Cookies

This site itself does not set cookies. However, if third-party advertising or analytics services are integrated (see below), those providers may use cookies or similar technologies.

4. Third-Party Services

This site uses the following third-party services:

5. Advertising

This site may display ads via Google AdSense. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads. You can manage your ad preferences at Google Ad Settings or opt out at aboutads.info.

6. Children's Privacy

This game is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children or any other users.

7. Your Rights

Since we do not collect personal data, there is no personal data to access or delete. Your high score is stored locally and can be cleared via your browser settings.

8. Policy Updates

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised date.
