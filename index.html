Brick Breaker — Free Online Arcade Game

Brick Breaker is a free classic arcade game you can play instantly in your browser — no download, no signup, no installation required. Smash through colorful brick walls using a paddle and ball across 5 challenging levels. Catch power-ups, build combos, and beat your high score!

How to Play

  • Move the paddle with your mouse, touch, or the left/right arrow keys.
  • Launch the ball by clicking, tapping, or pressing Space.
  • Break all bricks to clear the level and advance to the next one.
  • Don't let the ball fall — you have 3 lives. Lose them all and it's game over!
  • Catch power-ups that fall from broken bricks for special abilities.
  • Build combos by hitting multiple bricks without the ball touching the paddle — chain hits for bonus points!

Game Features

  • 5 unique levels — from simple walls to fortresses and diamond patterns, each with increasing difficulty.
  • 5 power-up types — Wide Paddle, Multi-Ball, Slow Motion, Extra Life, and Laser.
  • Combo system — chain consecutive brick hits for multiplied scores.
  • Particle effects — satisfying brick explosions with colorful debris.
  • Sound effects — retro arcade beeps generated in real-time (no audio files needed).
  • High score tracking — your best score is saved locally and persists between sessions.
  • Mobile-friendly — full touch controls support, responsive canvas, plays great on phones and tablets.

Power-Ups Guide

Power-ups drop randomly from broken bricks (about 12% chance). Catch them with your paddle to activate:

  • Wide Paddle (Green) — Makes your paddle 40% wider for 10 seconds. Great for tricky angles.
  • Multi-Ball (Blue) — Splits each active ball into three. More balls = more chaos = more points!
  • Slow Motion (Purple) — Slows the ball to half speed for 8 seconds. Easier to aim and react.
  • Extra Life (Red) — Gives you one additional life. The rarest and most valuable power-up.
  • Laser (Gold) — Charges your paddle with a laser beam for 6 seconds of offensive power.

Tips & Strategies

  • Aim the ball by hitting it with different parts of the paddle — edge hits create sharper angles.
  • Prioritize catching Extra Life and Multi-Ball power-ups — they have the biggest impact.
  • Build combos by keeping the ball bouncing among bricks without returning to the paddle.
  • On higher levels, focus on clearing bricks from the top first to keep the ball bouncing longer.
  • Use Slow Motion power-ups strategically when the ball gets too fast to track.
